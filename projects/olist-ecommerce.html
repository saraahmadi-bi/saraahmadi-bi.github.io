Project Overview

I started this project to strengthen my Python, SQL, and Power BI skills through one complete analytics workflow. Instead of practising each tool separately, I worked from the original marketplace files through data preparation, relational modeling, business analysis, and dashboard design.

The final Power BI report contains six interactive pages covering executive performance, sales and orders, customers, products and sellers, delivery and reviews, and geographic analysis. The report helps users follow marketplace performance and investigate the operational factors behind the results.

Data Source

The project uses the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist . It contains anonymized marketplace data for orders, customers, products, sellers, order items, payments, reviews, geolocation, and product-category translations.

The dataset covers orders placed between 2016 and 2018. Because September and October 2018 contain incomplete activity, monthly trend analysis ends in August 2018 to avoid presenting partial months as a business decline.

The data represents historical activity from the Olist marketplace and is intended for analytical and educational use. Customer and seller identifiers are anonymized.

Analysis Process

01

Python Data Preparation

I audited and cleaned nine source datasets with Python and Pandas. I checked missing values, duplicates, data types, date fields, and record consistency, then created analysis-ready features for delivery time, delivery performance, item count, product value, freight value, order totals, and payment differences.

02

MySQL Modeling & Analysis

I designed fact and dimension tables in MySQL, added primary and foreign keys, and tested referential integrity. I then wrote SQL queries to examine order status, revenue, monthly trends, product categories, seller performance, payment methods, delivery results, and repeat customers.

03

Power BI Development

I built the Power BI semantic model with a dedicated Date table and single-direction relationships. I created DAX measures and designed an interactive report with KPI cards, synchronized date filters, metric switching, cross-filtering, custom tooltips, bookmarks, Top-N analysis, Key Influencers, and Azure Maps.

Business Questions

Sales & Order Performance

The marketplace generated R$15.42 million in total sales from 99,441 orders, with an average order value of R$159.83. Monthly sales and order volume increased substantially through 2017 and remained comparatively strong during the complete months of 2018.

Credit cards accounted for 78.5% of payment value, while boleto represented 18.0%. This concentration makes card-payment reliability and payment-processing performance important to marketplace operations.

Customer Behavior

The analysis identified 96,096 unique customers, but only 3.1% were repeat customers. Average orders per customer remained close to one, showing that most customers purchased only once during the available period.

Customer activity was concentrated in major urban markets. São Paulo was the leading customer city, followed by Rio de Janeiro and Belo Horizonte.

Products & Sellers

The marketplace sold 110,197 items through 2,970 active sellers. Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, and Bed & Bath were the three leading product categories by sales.

Seller performance varied considerably. The scatter analysis compares items sold with product sales, while bubble size represents freight value, making it easier to identify high-volume sellers and sellers with higher delivery costs.

Delivery & Reviews

The overall delivery rate was 97.0%, and 93.2% of evaluated orders arrived on time. Average delivery time was 12.56 days, and the average review score was 4.09 out of 5.

Delivery performance had a clear relationship with customer feedback. On-time orders received far more five-star reviews, while late and non-delivered orders had substantially higher shares of one-star reviews.

Geographic Performance

São Paulo generated approximately R$5.77 million in customer sales, followed by Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais. The geographic report also maps the top 30 customer cities and the locations of the top 30 sellers by product sales.

A bookmark-based Cities and Sellers switch allows users to compare demand concentration with the geographic distribution of leading suppliers.

Business Recommendations

Olist should investigate the low repeat customer rate and test retention initiatives such as post-purchase communication, personalized product recommendations, and targeted offers for customers who completed their first successful order.

Delivery performance should remain a priority because late and non-delivered orders are strongly associated with low reviews. Seller-level monitoring can identify partners with recurring delays, unusually high freight costs, or weaker customer feedback.

Commercial planning should protect performance in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and other major markets while testing growth opportunities outside the strongest regions. Category strategy should prioritize leading categories while also considering seller capacity, freight cost, and delivery reliability.

Project Limitations

The dataset provides detailed historical marketplace activity but does not include several factors required for a complete commercial assessment:

  • Marketing campaigns and customer-acquisition costs
  • Website visits, browsing behavior, and conversion rates
  • Product cost, operating expenses, and net profitability
  • Inventory availability and seller fulfillment capacity
  • Customer demographics beyond the available location fields
  • Marketplace activity after the historical dataset period

September and October 2018 contain partial data, so monthly comparisons end in August 2018. The results describe the available historical records and should not be interpreted as a forecast of current marketplace performance.

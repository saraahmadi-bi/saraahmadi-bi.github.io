Project Overview

This end-to-end business intelligence project analyzes the performance of AdventureWorks, a fictional global bicycle company, to help decision-makers monitor sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic trends.

Developed as part of the Maven Analytics Power BI course, the project covers the complete BI workflow—from transforming raw CSV files and building a relational data model to creating DAX measures and designing a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

Data Source

The project uses the AdventureWorks raw dataset provided with the course, covering January 2020 to June 2022. It contains 56,046 sales records, 1,809 return records, 18,154 customers, 293 products, and 10 sales territories.

The source files include annual sales transactions, returns, and lookup tables for customers, products, product categories and subcategories, calendar dates, and geographic territories. Together, they support analysis across time, products, customers, and regions.

Analysis Process

01

Data Preparation

I imported the raw CSV files into Power BI and used Power Query to clean and transform the data. This included appending the annual sales files, promoting headers, correcting data types, handling inconsistent values, and preparing the customer, product, calendar, returns, and territory tables for analysis.

02

Data Modeling & DAX

I built a relational data model connecting the Sales and Returns fact tables to the supporting lookup tables through one-to-many relationships. I then created DAX measures for revenue, profit, orders, returns, target comparisons, previous-month performance, rolling trends, and pricing scenario analysis.

03

Dashboard Development

Using the completed model and DAX measures, I designed a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard covering executive performance, geographic sales, product analysis, and customer insights. I added KPI cards, slicers, navigation buttons, bookmarks, dynamic metric selection, and What-If analysis to support interactive exploration.

Business Questions

  • How did revenue, profit, order volume, and return rate perform over the analysis period?
  • Which product categories and individual products generated the most orders, revenue, and returns?
  • How did sales performance vary across countries, continents, and sales territories?
  • Who were the most valuable customers, and how did customer income and occupation relate to purchasing behavior?

Key Findings

$24.9M Total revenue generated from January 2020 to June 2022
$10.5M Total profit generated during the analysis period
25K Total customer orders recorded across all sales territories
2.2% Overall product return rate across AdventureWorks sales

Executive Performance

Between January 2020 and June 2022, AdventureWorks generated approximately $24.9 million in revenue, $10.5 million in profit, and 25,000 orders, while maintaining an overall return rate of 2.2%.

Revenue followed a clear upward trend, with stronger growth beginning in the second half of 2021. In the most recent month, revenue reached approximately $1.83 million, representing a 3.3% increase from the previous month.

Geographic Performance

AdventureWorks generated sales across North America, Europe, and the Pacific region. The United States represented the largest concentration of sales, followed by Australia, while Canada, the United Kingdom, France, and Germany contributed smaller shares.

Interactive continent filters allow users to isolate regional performance and compare sales activity across individual countries and territories.

Product and Pricing Analysis

Accessories recorded the highest category-level order volume at approximately 17,000 orders. The Water Bottle - 30 oz. ranked as the leading individual product by order volume, while Tires and Tubes represented the most frequently ordered product type. Shorts recorded the highest return volume.

The Product Details page compares monthly orders, revenue, and profit against their targets. A What-If parameter allows users to simulate price adjustments and evaluate their potential effect on product revenue and profitability.

Customer Insights

AdventureWorks served approximately 17,000 unique customers, generating an average of $1,400 in revenue per customer. Customers in the average-income segment placed the largest number of orders, while professionals represented the leading occupational group.

Mr. Maurice Shan was the highest-revenue customer in the selected analysis, generating approximately $12,408 from six orders. Interactive filters allow users to explore customer performance across different years and customer segments.

Business Recommendation

AdventureWorks should prioritize inventory and marketing support for its highest-demand product groups, particularly Accessories and Tires and Tubes. The company should also investigate the high return volume for Shorts to identify potential issues related to product quality, sizing, or customer expectations.

Product-level targets should be monitored regularly to identify underperforming products. For products such as the Water Bottle - 30 oz., which performed below its monthly order, revenue, and profit targets, the company could test targeted promotions, product bundles, or pricing adjustments before making broader changes.

Customer marketing should focus on the average-income and professional customer segments, which generated the highest order volumes. AdventureWorks should also strengthen retention strategies for high-value customers and continue developing its strongest geographic markets while evaluating growth opportunities in other regions.

Project Limitations

This project uses a fictional educational dataset and is intended to demonstrate the complete Power BI development workflow. Although the data supports detailed sales, product, customer, return, and geographic analysis, it does not include several factors required for real-world business decisions:

  • Marketing and promotional campaign costs
  • Inventory levels and product availability
  • Shipping, tax, and operational expenses
  • Competitor pricing and market conditions
  • Supplier and supply-chain performance
  • Real-time sales and customer activity

Profit calculations are based on the available product cost and revenue data and may not represent net profitability after all operating expenses. The findings should therefore be treated as a demonstration of analytical and dashboard-development skills rather than a final business strategy.
